## Frequently Asked

### Can I switch plans?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle. Pro-rated refunds for downgrades.

### Is there a free trial?

The Free tier is available forever with 1 workspace and 1 API key. Paid plans have 14-day free trials — no credit card required.

### What payment methods do you accept?

Credit cards via Stripe for monthly/annual billing. Enterprise plans can use invoicing with NET-30 terms.

### What happens if I go over limits?

We'll notify you before you hit limits. You can upgrade mid-cycle or we'll pause additional usage until the next period.
