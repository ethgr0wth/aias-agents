# For Founders & Startups

WhiteGlove helps you convert more visitors without hiring a personalization team. AI handles the complexity while you focus on your product.

## The Problem

Traditional websites show the same content to everyone. But your visitors are different:
- Some are technical, some aren't
- Some are ready to buy, some are just browsing
- Some need social proof, some need features

## The Solution

WhiteGlove watches how visitors behave and adapts your site in real-time:
- Developer hovering on docs? Show more code examples
- Visitor hesitating on pricing? Surface testimonials
- Someone scrolling fast? Simplify the content

## Results

- **Higher conversion rates** — Right message, right time
- **Lower bounce rates** — Visitors find what they need
- **Better engagement** — Personalized experience
