### Building for Florida's Climate

Central Florida construction requires specialized knowledge:

- **Hurricane-Rated Construction** — All homes meet Florida's strict wind codes
- **Concrete Block Construction** — Standard in Florida for durability
- **Impact Windows & Doors** — Hurricane protection built-in
- **Proper Ventilation** — Critical in Florida's humid climate
- **Termite Prevention** — Required treatments and construction methods
- **Energy Efficiency** — Designed for Florida's cooling demands
