### Post-Storm Safety Tips

After a storm hits your Central Florida home:

1. **Document everything** — Take photos before any cleanup
2. **Don't walk on the roof** — Let professionals assess safely
3. **Watch for mold** — Florida humidity means mold starts fast
4. **Call your insurance** — Report damage within 24 hours
5. **Call Rock Shield** — We'll secure and restore your property
